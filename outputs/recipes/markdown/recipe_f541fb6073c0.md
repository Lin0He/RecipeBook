# Pesto Pasta Salad

Loaded with fresh tomatoes, mozzarella, onion, and sliced basil, all tossed in a vibrant pesto sauce. Can use homemade kale pesto or your favorite store-bought. Top with shredded Parmesan cheese.

## Ingredients

- pasta
- tomatoes — fresh
- mozzarella
- onion
- basil — sliced
- pesto sauce — homemade kale or store-bought
- Parmesan cheese — shredded

## Steps

1. Cook pasta according to package directions. Drain and let cool.
2. Chop tomatoes, mozzarella, onion, and slice basil.
3. In a large bowl, combine cooked pasta, tomatoes, mozzarella, onion, and basil.
4. Add pesto sauce and toss until everything is well coated.
5. Top with shredded Parmesan cheese before serving.
